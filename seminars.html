ColMar Interactions Seminar

The seminar “Interactions” joins the groups in Cologne and Marburg, hence the name. It is a joint online seminar on recent developments in equivariant topology and equivariant symplectic geometry, organised by Silvia Sabatini, Oliver Goertsches and me. The current programme is here. To receive the login data, write to pako@mathematik.uni-marburg.de.
