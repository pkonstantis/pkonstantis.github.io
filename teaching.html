Teaching

Since 2013 I have given lectures and seminars at the universities of Marburg, Cologne, Gießen and Stuttgart, for students of mathematics, physics and the natural sciences and for future mathematics teachers. I write all my lecture notes myself in LaTeX.

Lectures and seminars

  • Winter 2024/25
    Justus-Liebig-Universität Gießen
    • Lecture Mathematik für Naturwissenschaftler (4 SWS)
      Contents

      Set theory, sequences and convergence, continuity, differentiability, integration, vector spaces, linear maps, eigenvalues, multivariable analysis, partial differential equations, numerical methods.

    • Lecture Morsetheorie (2 SWS)
      Contents

      Existence of Morse functions, gradient-like vector fields, Morse lemma, handle decompositions and their consequences.

  • Summer 2024
    Universität zu Köln
    • Lecture Algebraische Topologie 2 (4 SWS)
      Contents

      Cup product, Künneth formulas, homotopy groups and their computation, Hurewicz theorem, fibre bundles, stable homotopy groups, Postnikov towers, obstruction theory.

  • Summer 2023
    Philipps-Universität Marburg
    • Seminar Ricci flow (2 SWS)
      Contents

      Evolution of geometric quantities under the Ricci flow, maximum principle, parabolic PDEs and the Ricci flow, DeTurck trick, blow-ups, Perelman's W-functional, Uhlenbeck trick, Hamilton's theorem.

  • Winter 2022/23
    Justus-Liebig-Universität Gießen
    • Lecture Mathematik für Physiker (4 SWS)
      Contents

      Complex numbers, sequences, series, power series, convergence, continuity, differentiation, integration, vector spaces, linear maps, matrices, diagonalisation, eigenvalues and eigenspaces, scalar products, determinants, matrix groups.

    • Lecture Dirac-Operatoren in der Riemannschen Geometrie (2 SWS)
      Contents

      Clifford algebras, spin representation, connections on principal bundles, Riemannian geometry, differential forms, Clifford bundles, Dirac operators, spin structures, spinor bundles, analytic properties of the Dirac operator, Weitzenböck formulas, harmonic spinors.

  • Winter 2021/22
    Justus-Liebig-Universität Gießen
    • Lecture Mathematik für Physiker (4 SWS)

  • Summer 2021

  • Summer 2020
    Universität zu Köln

      Contents

      Knots and links, link diagrams, Reidemeister moves, Kauffman and Jones polynomials, colourability of knots, Alexander polynomial, knot groups, Seifert surfaces, Seifert matrices and the Alexander polynomial.

  • Winter 2019/20
    Universität zu Köln

  • Summer 2017
    Philipps-Universität Marburg

      Contents

      Morse theory, Milnor's proof of the h-cobordism theorem, existence of exotic spheres after Milnor and Brieskorn, the group structure of exotic structures on spheres.

      For future teachers. Intercept theorems, Pappus' theorem, theorems on triangles, Euler line, nine-point circle, theorems on quadrilaterals, hyperbolic geometry, isometries of the hyperbolic plane, geodesics, angle sums of hyperbolic triangles.
