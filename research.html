Research

My main research areas are transformation groups, geometric structures on manifolds, and geometric and differential topology.

Topics

  • GKM manifolds and GKM graphs
  • Hamiltonian non-Kähler actions
  • Symplectic and Kähler structures on biquotients
  • Geometric and topological invariants for maps from manifolds into spheres
  • Geometric and topological invariants for vector bundles over manifolds
  • Topological properties of G-structures on manifolds
  • Cappuccino spectra
