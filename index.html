About

Since October 2026 I have been a postdoc in the research group of Prof. Dr. Oliver Goertsches at the Department of Mathematics and Computer Science of the Philipps-Universität Marburg. Before that, my research there was funded by the Deutsche Forschungsgemeinschaft (DFG) through two individual research grants.

My research lies in transformation groups, geometric structures on manifolds and geometric and differential topology. Much of my recent work is on GKM theory and torus actions in symplectic and almost complex geometry.
